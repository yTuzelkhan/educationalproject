How it works

Up and running
in three steps.

01

Open the app

Click Launch Task Manager — no downloads, no accounts, no setup. The app opens instantly in your browser.

02

Add your tasks

Create tasks with a title, priority, category, due date and optional notes. Use search and filters to find anything fast.

03

Stay on track

Check off completed tasks, spot overdue items at a glance, and keep everything organized — all in one clean interface.
